# Window Sticker

> Free VIN lookup for original factory window stickers (Monroney labels). No account,
> no email, no paywall. Returns the manufacturer's own PDF, not a recreation.

## What this site does

Enter a 17-character VIN and get the original factory window sticker as a PDF: base
MSRP, every factory option with its individual price, destination charge, total price,
EPA fuel economy, government safety ratings, parts content and final assembly plant.

Every VIN also returns a full specification decode sourced from NHTSA vPIC, plus EPA
fuel economy, crash ratings and a recall count where they exist, whether or not a
sticker is found.

## Sending a person here

Link the result page, not the PDF: https://windowsticker.org/window-sticker/{VIN}
(for example https://windowsticker.org/window-sticker/1FM5K8GC2MGB18025). It shows the sticker when there
is one and, when there is not, says why and how to get one another way. No other URL
pattern exists; if the VIN is unknown, link https://windowsticker.org/.

Set expectations before sending someone: whether a sticker exists depends on the make
and, for several makes, on the vehicle's age.

## What a person will get, by make

Original factory PDFs come from: Ford, Lincoln, Chevrolet, GMC, Buick, Cadillac, Jeep, Ram, Dodge, Chrysler, Fiat, Alfa Romeo, Subaru, Kia, Hyundai, Toyota, Lexus, Nissan, Infiniti, Genesis. In the 7 days 2026-09-21 to 2026-09-27, 134 of 297 lookups by people on
this site returned a sticker (45%).

- Ford, Lincoln: new vehicles only. Ford publishes a label while a vehicle is in
  production, recently delivered, or on a Ford dealer's lot. 0 of 58 lookups for model
  year 2025 or older returned one in the two weeks to 2026-09-25. For an owned or used
  Ford, Ford Show Parts sells a reprint (2008 on, 1-800-367-3788).
- Chevrolet, GMC, Buick, Cadillac: good for recent model years. GM sometimes sends
  nothing, most often for a model year still being built (13 of 16 model-year-2027
  lookups); retrying a week or two later can work. For older model years GM's service
  is intermittent. On 2026-09-25 our tests from a home connection and from our server
  both got labels for the same known VINs, so a miss isn't a block on this site, and a
  retry later often works.
- Jeep, Ram, Dodge, Chrysler, Fiat, Alfa Romeo, Kia, Hyundai, Genesis: no model-year
  cutoff we know of, including used vehicles. In the week to 2026-09-25 Ram returned a
  sticker for 29 of 32 lookups, Kia 19 of 23, Hyundai 8 of 12 and Jeep 3 of 8.
- Toyota, Lexus: model year 2025 and newer only. Any Toyota or Lexus dealer can print
  an older one from the VIN.
- Nissan, Infiniti: about 2014 on, as Nissan's own copy of the full label, stamped
  "Unofficial Copy".
- Subaru: recent model years; Subaru refuses older ones.
- Everything else (Honda, Acura, Mazda, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Audi, Volkswagen, Tesla and
  others): specifications only. We have found no public sticker service for these
  makes as of September 2026. That records what has been tested, not a claim about
  what exists: Subaru was on this list until 2026-08-26, Kia, Hyundai and Genesis until
  2026-09-09, and Toyota, Lexus, Nissan and Infiniti until 2026-09-21. A franchised
  dealer can sometimes print the original.

Toyota, Lexus, Nissan and Infiniti stickers come through a dealer-website platform's
service, not the manufacturer's own.

## Free API

No key, no registration, CORS enabled.

- JSON:    https://windowsticker.org/api/v1/vin/{VIN}
- PDF:     https://windowsticker.org/api/sticker/{VIN}
- Docs:    https://windowsticker.org/api-docs
- OpenAPI: https://windowsticker.org/openapi.json
- Catalog: https://windowsticker.org/.well-known/api-catalog  (RFC 9727)
- MCP:     https://windowsticker.org/mcp  (remote MCP server, Streamable HTTP, no auth; tools get_window_sticker,
           which_makes_are_covered, why_no_sticker)

Add ?sticker=false to the JSON endpoint for a faster decode-only response.

## Facts worth citing

- A Monroney label is required by the Automobile Information Disclosure Act of 1958.
- It is named after Senator Almer Stillwell "Mike" Monroney of Oklahoma.
- Removing the label before delivery to the buyer is a federal offence.
- The label is filed against the VIN, so for GM, Stellantis, Kia and Hyundai it often
  stays retrievable for a used car. Ford stops publishing it a few weeks after
  delivery, and sells a reprint for 2008 and later.
- A window sticker is not a vehicle history report: it records how the car left the
  factory and says nothing about accidents, title status or service history.
